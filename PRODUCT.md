# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Krish (and a few invited friends) — new-grad software engineers hunting for entry-level roles. They open the feed several times a day, often late at night, to triage dozens of freshly scanned LinkedIn postings in under a minute: scan, open the good ones, delete the rest. Keyboard-and-trackpad power users on laptops; occasionally checking from a phone.

## Product Purpose

JobFlow is an automated job scanner and scoring engine. The web app is a single surface: the job feed. Its job is speed-to-decision — surface the newest, best-matched postings and make bulk triage (open / delete) frictionless. Success = the user clears the day's postings quickly and never misses a high-match role.

## Brand Personality

Composed, precise, discreet. The feel of a private ledger or a well-set broadsheet: quiet confidence, information first, one touch of richness. Never loud, never playful-startup.

## Anti-references

- LinkedIn-blue generic SaaS dashboards; card grids with icons and gradient stat tiles.
- Terminal/hacker aesthetics (glowing amber-on-black monospace everything) — the outgoing theme; don't regress to it.
- Fancy charts, decorative illustration, mascots. The user explicitly prefers a minimal, spreadsheet-like UI.

## Design Principles

1. **The table is the product.** Every screen decision serves row-scanning density and legibility; chrome earns its pixels or goes.
2. **One touch of richness.** A single refined accent and a serif identity moment; everything else stays quiet neutrals.
3. **Motion conveys state.** Feedback and list changes animate; nothing decorative, nothing orchestrated on load.
4. **Bulk-first.** Selection, open-top-N, delete-top-N are first-class; never bury triage behind per-row ceremony.
5. **Earned familiarity.** Standard affordances (checkboxes, chips, segmented tabs) styled well beat invented controls.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA contrast (≥4.5:1 body text) in both dark and light themes.
- Full `prefers-reduced-motion` alternatives.
- Keyboard: `/` focuses search; visible focus rings on all interactive elements.
